# Level 3 recorder and an L3-10 run (T-005)

Date: 2026-10-08, 09:10 to 09:45. Recorder: `e2e/record.py`. Logged out. Recordings in `~/purple-recordings/2026-10-08-channel-a`, `-channel-a-2` and `2026-10-08-channel-b-5` (outside the repo). Probe: [`record_manifest_probe.py`](probes/record_manifest_probe.py).

## Recorder

`python e2e/record.py <channel|-> --seconds N [--with-purple] [--technique TR-xxx] [--fresh-profile] [--segment-bodies]`: Edge in record mode (Purple off) or extension mode, CDP `Fetch` at the response stage on usher, `*.m3u8`, `/v1/segment/`, `gql.twitch.tv/gql` and `edge.ads.twitch.tv`, each paused request continued in a task of its own. `manifest.json` has every entry with its offset from the start, the full URL and the status; masters, media playlists, `PlaybackAccessToken` calls and `edge.ads` responses keep their bodies; segments keep URL, status and type, and are marked `ad`, `live` or not listed after the media playlists recorded in the session. The `summary` has the usher query with `token`, `sig`, `play_session_id` and `p` left out.

Interception side effects seen while writing it:

- With `Fetch` on, `Page.navigate` from `about:blank` to a channel never returned (30 s, three times on the dedicated profile), with every paused request continued. After one load of the directory before `Fetch` was turned on, the same navigation returned at once and the video played. The recorder loads the directory first.
- Handling a paused request inside the event handler, awaiting `Fetch.getResponseBody`, also stalled the session; each one runs in its own task.
- Reading each segment body over CDP is left out by default: live segments come without `content-length` (chunked), so their size is not in the manifest unless the bodies are kept.

## Effect on the e2e checks

L3-02 and the soak report count ad media from the ad segment lines of the playlists the player got (`adSegmentPaths` in `e2e/worker-logger.js`). On an fMP4 stream with prefetch lines, the player fetches the prefetch URIs, which differ from the segment lines, so an ad segment fetched through its prefetch line does not match its segment line. Prerolls have no prefetch lines (B-026), and the announced-break prefetch lines were checked by URI in the [midroll soak](2026-10-08-midroll-soak.md); a midroll keeps its prefetch lines (B-028). Since this finding, `adSegmentPaths` also holds the `EXT-X-MAP` before an ad segment and the prefetch lines after an ad segment or after a stitched-ad marker past the last segment (`e2e/worker-logger.spec.ts`).
